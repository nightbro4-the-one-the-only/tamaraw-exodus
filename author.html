The Author's Message

Why this world exists

Dear Reader,

Thank you for opening this page as well as taking a look at what I've made. Before you read the reasons below, know that the first one is that I always wanted to create stories about the Philippine. Second is that I wanted to leave behind something in this world. A memory, a gift and a reminder of the ideas I hold dear. Agree or disagree with my words I am just glad to leave something behind.

Why I Created This

This world began as a simple idea that I had when I was still in university. About magic and how it can actually work out. The hydrostones, the firestone and more were the first ones that I've created. Then it ballooned when I got into roleplaying. Then got into post apocalyptic and then I fell in love with my pilipino roots. Then it all ended up with this massive world that I ended up committing to. Its my first story and from this everything evolved to become much more developed as I got people to look at it and give me insights on what I was doing wrong and what I was doing write. Quite the long ride but, damn was it a fun and eye opening one.

Thank You

Designing all of the creatures was one of my favorites and yep they have alot of pilipino myths mixed in which after researching I found to be quite the interesting idea. Anyway if you are reading this I just want to say that I am grateful and glad that you would take a look at the chaos that I call my writing. Be safe out there and hope that you all have a great life and thank you.

— Jazzy

I dedicate the writing here to my nation the Philippine. My muse. My nation that I belovingly live in. Its people one of a kind. Its land diverse and a sight to behold. The pearl of the orient with stories as colorful as its sails. Young as you may and troubled is your start. I pray, I believe, and I have faith that in the future my people will not just endure but, prosper with kindness in their hearts and accepting as we were raised to be. Long live my beloved nation and I hope, truly hope. That one day the world will respect and love you.
