TAMARAW EXODUS

The wars did not end the world. The world ended itself — and then gave mankind three years to face the truth. In the ruins of Manila, a continent that should be myth has risen from the sea, bearing a gate to the unknown. And the last fleet of Earth is being built, ship by salvaged ship, to carry what remains of the Philippines into it.
