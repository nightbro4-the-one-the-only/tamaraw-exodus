How the world moves

Cause, Reaction — and Time

Nothing in this world is instant. Every action is a cause; every consequence is a reaction that arrives on its own day. These are the rules that make it feel real.

Nothing Lands on the Same Day

Every action is a ticket with a due date. A farm takes days to build; a road network takes months; travelling between towns takes hours. The world records what you started and resolves it when the time comes — whether you are watching or not.

Consequences Arrive Late

A battle resolves in a day; the news reaches the next nation in two; the embargo bites in three; the refugees arrive in five. Cause and reaction are never the same event — you always get to see the gap between them.

The World Tells You Before It Happens

"The siege continues — if unbroken, the city falls in four days." You always see what is coming before it lands, and you have exactly that long to act on it.

Every Scale, One World

A person crafts a knife in hours; a guild raises a workshop in weeks; a nation musters an army in months. Your character's prices, safety, and news are shaped by their nation — rippling down through the same clock.

Before you begin

Pick a Date, Pick a Scale

The Story page is the canon. The Lore page is the world. Here, you choose when to enter it — and from how far away. Every story below is a different size of the same world, on the same clock.

A Soldier of the Long War

Begin in Era I, as one life among millions. The war is all you have ever known — what you fight for, who you refuse to fight, and what you are willing to spend are yours to decide.

Scale: one person.

A Captain of a Militia Ship

Begin in the Exodus era, as the master of one vessel in the forming fleet. Your crew, your cargo, your conscience — and the directive that says the ship comes first.

Scale: a crew, a ship, a small settlement.

A Nation Building Its Fleet

Begin in the Three Years, as a nation deciding what to carry through the gate — and what to leave behind. Every choice is measured in ships, seeds, and the people who will not fit.

Scale: a nation.

A Stayer in a Temple

Begin as one of the ones who chose faith over the fleet. The gate is a lie; the rapture is coming; the world is ending, and you are certain you will live forever. The story has not decided whether you are right.

Scale: one shrine — or a world that chose to stay.
